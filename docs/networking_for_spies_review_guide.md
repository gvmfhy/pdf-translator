# Bilingual Review Guide

Review the assigned Russian source chunk against the English translation in full.

## Priorities

1. Correct omissions, additions, reversals, and factual or logical mistranslations.
2. Correct names, quotations, numbers, examples, acronyms, headings, lists, and captions.
3. Replace misleading calques with natural English while preserving the authors' meaning and direct, instructional voice.
4. Preserve paragraph order and all substantive content. Do not summarize or embellish.
5. Preserve Markdown headings and useful list structure. Remove only chunk-processing artifacts, not book content.

## House Style and Terminology

- Title: *Networking for Spies: How to Benefit from Any Acquaintance*.
- Authors: Elena Vavilova and Andrey Bezrukov.
- `разведка`: intelligence; `разведчик`: intelligence officer or spy according to context.
- `разведчик-нелегал`: deep-cover ("illegal") intelligence officer on first formal use; deep-cover intelligence officer thereafter where natural.
- `оперативный работник`: intelligence officer or operative, never "operative worker."
- `оперативная задача`: operational objective/task.
- `объект разработки`: target or cultivation target, according to context; avoid the calque "development target" where it obscures the meaning.
- `источник`: source, when used in the intelligence sense.
- `связи`: contacts, connections, or relationships according to context; do not force one English word everywhere.
- `личная социальная сеть`: personal social network.
- `внедрение`: entry, establishing oneself, or penetration according to context; preserve quotation marks when the Russian marks the term.
- Use idiomatic American English, consistent with the existing translation, and use standard English double quotation marks.
- Keep book-page references and internal cross-references accurate. Do not invent citations.

## Editing and Report

Edit only the assigned `translations/chunk_NNN_translation.md` file. Use `apply_patch` for edits. Re-read the completed file, then report:

- whether every Russian page in the chunk is represented;
- all material corrections, grouped as critical/major/minor;
- any uncertain passage that needs root review;
- important terminology decisions that later chunks should follow.
